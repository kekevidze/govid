Government and Major Banks Launch AI System to Support Supplementary Universal Income for Australians

Over 142,000 users across Australia have registered for this AI-powered trading system. With ASIC-regulated protection, robust investor safeguards, and up to 94% accuracy, early users report consistent weekly earnings with just 45 minutes daily.

Australian residents are quietly earning $450–$850 per week with a new AI-powered trading platform that's just opened to the public. With over 142,000 users across Australia already registered, this could be your chance to explore the platform.

Why This Australian Launch Matters Now

While ChatGPT and other AI tools dominate headlines, a quieter revolution has been brewing: artificial intelligence designed specifically to simplify online income for Australian citizens, not just tech experts or wealthy investors.

This government project has just officially launched across Australia, bringing automated tools to ordinary Australians. It is designed to help users explore trading strategies with minimal time investment.

How It Actually Works

The government project connects users to an AI system that scans global financial markets, analysing currencies, commodities, and indices in real time. Instead of spending hours studying charts, the AI identifies potential opportunities and provides signals.

Once you've registered, the system begins monitoring markets. You can adjust risk settings, pause activity, or withdraw funds; the platform handles analysis while you make the final decisions.
